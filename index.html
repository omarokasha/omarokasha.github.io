  • Vaccine safety · consensus

    Risk windows for adverse events of special interest

  • Evidence synthesis · global

    Burden of pneumococcal and Hib disease

    Systematic reviews and meta-analysis of complicated pneumococcal pneumonia in the era of higher-valent PCVs, and of the re-emergence of invasive H. influenzae type b in Europe, 2010–2024. Further reviews on IPD in adults, invasive E. coli disease and RSV.

    • Eur J Clin Microbiol Infect Dis, 2025 ↗

    Risk management plan and PASS planning for a COVID-19 vaccine

    Wrote epidemiology sections of an EU risk management plan and assessed PASS feasibility in European data networks for a vaccine developer.

  • Prospective cohort · Sub-Saharan Africa

    Incidence of P. falciparum malaria

    Multi-country prospective cohort with primary data collection in the general population aged 5–60 years, supported by a narrative review of the literature.

  • Study design · the Americas

    Norovirus gastroenteritis

    Protocol development for a prospective cohort using real-world data in older adults in the US, and for a household transmission study in Latin America.

  • Feasibility & database protocols

    Pneumonia, otitis media and meningococcal disease

    Database protocols for pneumonia and lower respiratory tract infection incidence from ICD-coded data, a US multi-state feasibility assessment for pneumococcal VE against otitis media, and a study of meningococcal disease sequelae in Latin America.

2014 – 2019

Doctoral Researcher, Vaccine Epidemiology

Medical Epidemiologist

US Naval Medical Research Unit No. 3 (NAMRU-3), Cairo · Infection Control Unit, with US CDC

Full-time 2011–2013, then remote consultant until 2016. Built capacity at the Egyptian Ministry of Health and Population to prevent and control healthcare-associated infections, including sentinel surveillance for surgical site infections.

  • National policy · Egypt
